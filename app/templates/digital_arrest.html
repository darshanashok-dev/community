How Digital-Arrest Scams Work

Based on Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) Advisory TAU/ADV/003 and CERT-In guidance, scammers combine authority impersonation with psychological manipulation:

1. Government & Regulator Impersonation

Calls often begin with an automated or live claim from TRAI, Telecom Dept, Customs, Courier, or RBI alleging your SIM card, Aadhaar, or parcel is flagged for illegal activity.

2. Police, CBI & Court Impersonation

You are "transferred" to fake Cyber Crime, Police, CBI, or Enforcement Directorate officers who display forged badges, FIRs, or arrest warrants with official-looking emblems.

3. Fear & Artificial Urgency

Scammers cite serious offences such as money laundering or narcotics trafficking and insist you must settle or "verify" immediately before police arrive at your door.

4. Video-Call Pressure & Surveillance

Victims are told to stay continuously on Skype or WhatsApp video—sometimes facing a staged police-station backdrop—and forbidden from disconnecting the call.

5. Secrecy & Isolation Instructions

They instruct you to lock your room and not tell family, friends, or bank staff under the pretext of a "confidential national-security inquiry" so nobody can warn you.

6. 'Safe-Account' Payment Scams

Finally, they demand you transfer funds to an "RBI verification", "escrow", or "government safe" account, falsely promising a refund once your account is cleared.

The Three-Stage Psychological Manipulation Cycle

Research on digital-arrest coercion highlights three connected stages used to overwhelm rational verification:

Stage 1: Initiation

Authority Trigger

An unexpected call uses official titles, case numbers, or your name/phone details to trigger shock and deference to authority.

Stage 2: Manipulation

Fear, Urgency & Isolation

Continuous video surveillance, threats of arrest, and strict secrecy prevent you from pausing to speak with family or real authorities.

Stage 3: Compliance

Financial Extraction

Exhausted and frightened victims are guided to break fixed deposits or send UPI/RTGS transfers to "prove innocence" or fund a "safe account".
