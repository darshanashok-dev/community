Six Common Tactics Used in Digital-Arrest Scams

Based on Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) and CERT-In advisories, scammers commonly combine these psychological pressures:

1. Authority Impersonation

Callers claim to represent Police, CBI, Customs, Narcotics, RBI, TRAI, or courier firms, citing fake case numbers or forged IDs.

2. Fear & Artificial Urgency

They allege your Aadhaar, SIM, or parcel is linked to money laundering or contraband and demand immediate action to avoid arrest.

3. Video-Call Pressure

Victims are instructed to keep their camera on continuously on messaging or video apps, sometimes in front of a fake police-station backdrop.

4. Secrecy Instructions

Scammers forbid you from speaking to family, friends, or bank staff—claiming the investigation is confidential—to prevent a reality check.

5. 'Safe-Account' Transfers

You are pressured to move savings into a "RBI verification" or "government safe" account with the promise it will be refunded after clearance.

6. Isolation & Exhaustion

Long calls wear down judgement. Hanging up and verifying independently with family or local police immediately breaks their control.
