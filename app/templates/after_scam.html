  1. 1

    Call 1930 immediately for financial cyber fraud

    If money was transferred or banking credentials were shared, dial India's National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 as quickly as possible so suspicious fund flows can be flagged.

  2. 2

    Report through cybercrime.gov.in

    File an official complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and save your acknowledgement number.

  3. 3

    Contact your bank or payment provider immediately

    Use the official customer care number on the back of your card or inside your verified bank app to freeze compromised cards, UPI access, or net-banking credentials.

  4. 4

    Preserve transaction records

    Save UTR/reference numbers, timestamps, recipient account/UPI IDs, and bank SMS alerts.

  5. 5

    Retain relevant messages and evidence

    Take screenshots of suspicious SMS, WhatsApp chats, caller numbers, video-call details, and any URLs or forged documents sent to you.

  6. 6

    Follow up through official channels only

    Track your complaint via cybercrime.gov.in or your local cyber police station. Never pay any third party claiming they can "recover" lost funds for a fee.
