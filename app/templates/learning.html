Official Reporting & Awareness Resources

Verified Indian cybercrime reporting and advisory portals.

  • National Cyber Crime Helpline — Dial 1930

    24/7 toll-free national helpline for immediate reporting of online financial fraud so banks can attempt to freeze disputed transactions (CFCFRMS).

  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal — cybercrime.gov.in

    Official Government of India (I4C / Ministry of Home Affairs) portal for reporting digital-arrest threats, phishing, financial cyber fraud, and tracking complaint acknowledgements.

  • Indian Computer Emergency Response Team — cert-in.org.in

    Publishes official cybersecurity advisories on online scams (such as CIAD-2024-0050) and handles technical security incident reporting for organisations.

  • After-Scam Ordered Recovery Workflow

    Open our 6-step post-scam assistance checklist for calm guidance and evidence preparation.
