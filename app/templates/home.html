How this platform protects your privacy

  • In-memory message checks: Pasted texts are never saved to a database or shared with third-party services.
  • No link visiting: Submitted URLs are examined strictly as text—never opened, followed, or fetched.
  • Advisory, not absolute: "No known red flags" never guarantees a message, caller, or link is safe.
  • Calm, non-blaming support: If you suspect a scam, you are not to blame. Clear recovery steps are always one click away.
