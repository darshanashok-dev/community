# Voluntary Usability Evaluation Workflow (20–30 Participants)

> **Status:** Study protocol and questionnaire templates prepared; human participant evaluation has **not** been conducted yet and no participant responses are fabricated.

## 1. Ethics, Consent & Privacy Rules

- **Target sample:** Approximately **20–30 voluntary adult participants** (including non-technical users and, where feasible, older participants alongside RNSIT/community volunteers).
- **Informed consent:** Explain clearly before starting that:
  - Participation is completely voluntary and may be stopped at any time.
  - Only **fictional demonstration scenarios** are used during tasks.
  - Participants must **not** enter real personal messages, OTPs, phone numbers, bank details, or personal identifiers.
  - Feedback is recorded anonymously using a random participant code (`P01`–`P30`).
- **Scope caveat:** Results are indicative usability observations only and do not prove long-term behaviour change or causation.

## 2. Informed Consent Statement (Read / Provided Before Session)

> "You are invited to test an academic cybersecurity awareness web application developed by CSE students at RNS Institute of Technology. During this session, you will review fictional sample messages, URLs, checklists, and awareness pages. No personal identifiers, real messages, or contact details will be collected. Your anonymous ratings help us improve clarity and usability. You may stop at any time."

## 3. Scenario-Based Tasks

1. **Task 1 (Phishing SMS):** Paste the provided fictional KYC phishing SMS into the Message Analyser and identify the top two reasons the score is elevated.
2. **Task 2 (Benign Bank Alert):** Paste the provided fictional genuine bank debit/OTP alert and observe whether the platform avoids a false high-risk alarm.
3. **Task 3 (URL Inspection):** Inspect the provided fictional look-alike URL without clicking it, and state who controls the actual registrable domain.
4. **Task 4 (Digital-Arrest Call Checklist):** Complete the call checklist for a fictional video-call threat scenario and locate the `1930` / `cybercrime.gov.in` reporting steps.
5. **Task 5 (Android Permissions & Quiz):** Find where to review *Install unknown apps* in the Android guide and complete the 8-question awareness quiz.

## 4. Anonymous Questionnaire

Collect anonymous responses in [usability_questionnaire_template.csv](usability_questionnaire_template.csv) covering:
- Clarity of score explanations (1–5 Likert)
- Ease of navigation on mobile/desktop (1–5 Likert)
- Calmness and non-blaming tone of After-Scam page (1–5 Likert)
- Understanding of "advisory only / not proof of safety" notices (Yes/No/Partial)
- Task completion & qualitative usability observations.
